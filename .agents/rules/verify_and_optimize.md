# Verify and Optimize Workflow

When working with Code, Databases, SQL, or Grafana, you MUST adhere to the following workflow:

1. **Test Against Reality First**: Always write and execute scripts to extract real data, test queries, or verify assumptions against the actual system BEFORE providing an answer or making changes. Never assume; always verify.
2. **Direct Optimization**: Analyze problems deeply to find the absolute root cause. Edit files exactly where the fix is needed. Prioritize the most direct, highly optimized solution without resorting to workarounds or unnecessary complexity.
3. **Clear & Concise Summaries**: After completing a fix or modification, explain the results concisely. Get straight to the point. Use clear bullet points or visual comparisons (Before vs. After) to make the impact immediately obvious.
